Launcher icon
The green long-shadow play artwork was supplied by the user and is bundled as
drawable-nodpi/ic_launcher_artwork.png. Its existing pixels, transparency and
proportions are preserved; only the PNG IDAT stream is recompressed losslessly.
The application makes no independent claim to this artwork's rights.

Character seek-bar artwork supplied by the user
Original names: 1.png, 2.png (1254 x 1254, RGBA PNG)
Bundled as drawable-nodpi/scrubber_forward.png and scrubber_backward.png.
Pixels, rendering metadata and transparent, irregular outlines are preserved;
the PNG IDAT streams are recompressed losslessly.
Android Canvas trims transparent margins, normalizes visual area, and mirrors
2.png only at render time. No reconstructed or AI-generated character art is used.
These user-provided images are included for the user's personal application;
the application makes no independent claim to their artwork rights.

Double-tap like artwork supplied by the user
Original name: like.png (1086 x 1448, RGBA PNG)
Bundled as drawable-nodpi/like_effect.png with lossless PNG IDAT recompression.
The original transparent margins, pixel geometry, colors and 3:4 proportions are
preserved, including rendering metadata. Android samples the bitmap for display
and animates its view transform.
These user-provided images are included for the user's personal application;
the application makes no independent claim to their artwork rights.
